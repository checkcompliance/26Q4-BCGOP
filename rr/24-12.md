# RONR 24:12

**Taking the vote on an appeal**

> The vote is taken so that the affirmative will be in favor of sustaining the chair’s decision, as follows: CHAIR: Those in favor of sustaining the chair’s decision, say aye.… Those opposed to sustaining this decision, say no.…
