RONR 24:10

Stating the question on an appeal

The chair, after stating clearly the exact question at issue, and the reasons for his decision if he thinks an explanation necessary, states the question on the appeal as follows: CHAIR: The question is: “Shall the decision of the chair stand as the judgment of the assembly [or “club,” “society,” “board,” etc.]?” Or: CHAIR: The question is, “Shall the decision of the chair be sustained?”
