RONR 59:34

Two-thirds vote for a rules package

A group of proposed standing rules requires a two-thirds vote for its adoption whenever it includes any rule that would require a two-thirds vote for its adoption if voted on individually (see next paragraph below). Therefore, under the usual procedure of voting on the standing rules as a “package,” a two-thirds vote will normally be required for their adoption— because, if they are to fill the needs of the convention, they nearly always include provisions that can be imposed only by a two-thirds vote.
