RONR 59:36

Amending or rescinding a convention standing rule

Vote Required to Amend or Rescind a Convention Standing Rule. To amend or rescind a standing rule of a convention requires a two-thirds vote or the vote of a majority of all the delegates or other “voting members” of the convention who have been registered, except that a rule individually adoptable by a majority vote can be amended or rescinded by a majority vote after notice on at least the preceding day.
